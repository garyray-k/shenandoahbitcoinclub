About

Bitcoin is hope.

Have you heard about Bitcoin recently? Do you want to know how it is different from and superior to the rest of the other cryptocurrencies?

Already deep in the rabbit hole, enjoying your discovery of this revolutionary technology?

Whatever level of knowledge or interest you have in Bitcoin, you are welcome to join us. Come meet fellow Bitcoiners in the Northern Shenandoah area — share, learn, and have fun!

See you soon.

When and Where

We hold two monthly meetups:

  • Harpers Ferry Bitcoin Meetup — 1st Saturday, Appalachian Snail, Harpers Ferry, WV
  • Shenandoah Bitcoin Club — 3rd Saturday, Daily Buzz Cafe, Middletown, VA

We do not offer financial or legal advice at our club.

If you're interested in more information, please reach out.
